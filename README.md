# Duck Hunts: The Ducks Strike Back

A retro, NES-style browser game where the duck has the gun. Fly over a scrolling
field, shoot the hunters, or swoop in with the knife.

**Play it:** https://avgunaa.github.io/duck-hunts/

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard / mouse | Touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fly | Arrow keys or WASD | D-pad |
| Fire current weapon | Click | A button, or tap the screen |
| Knife dive (45° the way the duck faces) | Space or right-click | B button |
| Drop an egg bomb | E | EGG |
| Switch weapon | Q, 1/2/3 or mouse wheel | WEAPON |
| Pause | P | PAUSE |
| Mute / CRT lines / Zapper flash | M / C / F | |

## How it works

- Each round has 10 hunters. Hit enough of them to meet the quota, then beat the boss
  to move on.
- Later rounds scroll faster and send more hunters at once (up to 5).
- Hunters hide behind tree trunks and up in the leaves, so watch for falling leaves.
- Special hunters: Bubba (shield), Gus (disguised as a bush), Boomer (dynamite) and
  Ron (jetpack).
- Weapons: shotgun, egg bombs (blast, beats shields) and a zapper beam that goes
  through everyone in its line.
- Bosses rotate: Big Rig Randy, Chopper Chuck, Mecha-Earl 9000 and B-2 Bomber Bob
  (homing missiles that give up after 3 seconds). They aim smarter in later rounds.
- Running away doesn't help: hunters sprint and fire faster, and bosses go into a
  short turbo charge.
- The camera pulls the duck back to the centre: the further out you fly, the faster
  the world scrolls.
- Chain hits for a combo multiplier, and pop balloons for hearts, ammo, a shield or
  double score.
- High scores are stored in your own browser.

## Running locally

The whole game is `index.html`. Open it in any browser; there is nothing to build.
